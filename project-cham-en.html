Step 1

From market opportunity to value proposition

Across 5 team interviews, the hard part was not choosing a venue but coordinating: bookings, timing, gifts and setting all have to fit together. Spending on special occasions remains strong, but each competitor only handles one piece.

01 · Market opportunity
56.4%spend VND 201–500k per person on special occasions
88%eat out for birthdays or anniversaries; 64.4% choose restaurants (iPOS 2024)
52.5%eat out less often, saving their budget for special occasions
02 · Competitor gap
PlanningĐi Hẹn: suggests ideas, but customers book themselves
Bookings, ticketsKlook, Ticketbox: single steps only
All-inclusiveThe F Lab, TIFF: premium services
03 · Value proposition

A single point of coordination

  • Checks availability for customers
  • One request code to track progress
  • Customers keep the final say
Step 2

From request to confirmation: one flow, one code

1Choose a package

Curated or Personalised

2Share preferences

3Review & pay

Enter the code to see the status

6Review & request changes

Send feedback on the concept

Lesson

Coordination is part of the experience too: customers need to know who is handling what, and where their request is.

Limitations

Not yet tested with real customers. The biggest uncertainty: willingness to pay VND 359K–659K.
