Vinamilk 100%

A marketing plan for Vinamilk 100% UHT fresh milk: from reading the market and customers to an integrated communication campaign.

Team of 7 · Team leaderAcademic project09/2026
Research goal

Vinamilk 100% is the easiest milk to buy, but do customers have a clear reason to choose it?

My role

  • Team leader, coordinating 7 members
  • Worked on product, 4P, environment, SWOT–TOWS, segmentation and positioning analysis
  • Helped restructure the campaign phases

Methods

  • 4P, micro and macro environment analysis
  • SWOT → TOWS to choose a strategic direction
  • Segmentation, targeting, positioning

Data & tools

  • InsightAsia 2025 survey, Euromonitor 2026
  • Actual retail prices of 3 brands
  • Vinamilk annual reports

Key results

  • Gap: easy to buy, but no clear reason to choose
  • Target: health-conscious urban adults
  • Positioning: Health × Flavour

Key insight

Ease of purchase only explains where customers buy Vinamilk, not why they should choose Vinamilk 100% over other brands.

Step 2

Choosing customers and a position

01

Health-conscious urban adults

Primary target. 31% of the market, buy 4.5 times a week, willing to pay 25–40% more. Currently prefer TH true MILK (72%) over Vinamilk Green Farm (58%).

02

Value-seeking families

Secondary target. 28% of the market, 91% choose Vinamilk for value. Keeping them protects volume, even though they are price-sensitive and switch for promotions.

03

Young convenience seekers

Served through existing channels. 18% of the market, low loyalty, often switching between 2–3 brands. No dedicated investment needed.

Positioning direction

Don't copy TH's natural, premium position. Combine three things Vinamilk already has (ease of purchase, flavour variety and trust), then add a clear health reason: Health × Flavour, accessible every day.

Value proposition: more benefits for the same price.

Key insight

Customers are not short of places to buy Vinamilk. What they lack is a clear enough reason to pay full price.

Step 3

From insight to campaign

Big idea · 16/10 – 31/12/2026

“Đa Vị Việt, Đủ Chất Vươn Xa” (Vietnamese Flavours, Nourishment to Go Further)

Each flavour is linked to a Vietnamese region and cultural feature; nutrition is the shared foundation. Goals: raise premium perception from 56% to 70%, have at least 40% of viewers name a specific reason to choose Vinamilk 100%, and grow full-price sales instead of relying on deep discounts.

Phase 1 · Engagement

Turn buying into an experience.

  • MVLaunch the “Đa Vị Việt” story on YouTube, TikTok and Facebook
  • Packaging14 pack designs linking flavours to Vietnamese landscapes
  • PhotoboothBuy 2 multipacks, get a QR ticket, take a free photo with a campaign frame
Phase 2 · Amplify

Turn customers' experiences into stories that spread.

  • UGCCustomers share photobooth pictures with the campaign hashtag
  • CurationVinamilk reposts real stories, with permission
  • OOH, PRBring the stories to billboards, LED screens and the press
My role in this part

The campaign idea came from my teammates. I helped restructure the phases so each one has a clear job and leads into the next: buy, experience, then share.

Lesson

A brand's biggest strength isn't necessarily why customers choose it. “Is it easy to buy?” has to be separated from “why do they choose us?”.

Limitations

This is desk research from secondary sources. Many figures are measured at the Vinamilk brand level, not for the 100% line alone. The campaign is a proposal; the KPIs and the VND 4.884 billion budget are untested estimates.
