MoMo

Comparing the digital communication performance of e-wallet MoMo against its direct competitor ZaloPay.

IndividualAcademic project11/2025
Research goal

Evaluate MoMo's integrated communication performance against ZaloPay.

My role

  • Worked independently
  • Compared data for two brands
  • Drew recommendations for MoMo

Methods

  • Direct competitor analysis
  • Integrated communication review
  • Social media and website comparison

Data & tools

  • Data from Aug–Oct 2025
  • MoMo vs ZaloPay
  • Public reports and documents

Key results

  • MoMo leads on reach and site visits
  • Engagement fell more sharply than ZaloPay's
  • 3 recommendations: content, personalisation, retention
Step 1

Wide reach, but low engagement

MetricMoMoZaloPay
Followers1.03M833k
Posts8361
Engagement (change)23.7k (−92%)28.7k (−58.3%)
Website visits11.52M9.79M
Bounce rate52.21%27.11%
Pages per visit2.012.91

Source: SimilarWeb, Rival IQ, Aug–Oct 2025.

Key insight

MoMo leads on reach, but its engagement fell more sharply and web visitors leave faster.

Step 2

Three recommendations for MoMo

  1. Optimise content to lift engagement: less diluted, closer to what users care about.
  2. Prioritise mobile personalisation to improve retention and conversion.
  3. Learn how ZaloPay keeps users exploring deeper, without relying on discounts.
Lesson

A single metric on its own can mislead; read it alongside other metrics and the context.

Limitations

Website metrics are only an indirect signal for an app-first product. Engagement rates also tend to fall as follower counts grow.
